10:00 AMThe idea

One page for the professional. One place for the client.

Slotify has two sides. A professional sets up a practice page with services, hours and rules. A client finds that page by its link name, sees only the times that are really free, and books one.

It is made for people who work by appointment: advocates, doctors, consultants, tutors, therapists, freelancers and small businesses. The scheduling engine knows nothing about any one profession. Wording, services, extra questions, hours and rules are all settings, so the same app fits a clinic and a tutoring practice.

11:00 AMHow it works

From an empty account to a booked appointment.

In the order it really happens.

  1. 1Set up your practice

    Add your services, each with a duration, an optional price and optional questions for the client. Choose working days and hours, a daily break, days off, how much notice you need, how far ahead people can book, and a daily limit.

  2. 2Get reviewed, then go live

    New professional pages are reviewed before clients can see them. You are notified of every decision, with the reviewer’s note if there is one.

  3. 3Share your link name

    Your page shows your photo, services, hours, booking policy, contact details and social links, and a track record. Copy your link or share it on WhatsApp from the dashboard.

  4. 4Clients pick a time

    A client chooses a service, then a date and a time from live availability, answers your questions and sees the price, or “Free”. If you have turned payments on, they can pay at this step.

  5. 5You respond, everyone is reminded

    Confirm instantly or review each request. Notifications arrive in the app and on the phone, with a reminder an hour before. Clients can reschedule or cancel until your cut-off.

    2:00 PMTry the rules

    Change a rule. Watch the day change.

    The same steps the app takes to work out open times: your hours, the break, existing bookings, the gap after each appointment, minimum notice and the daily limit. The bookings and times below are sample data, and nothing here is saved.

    More ways to set it up

    • A freelancer who wants a deposit. Switch on online payments and take a deposit at booking. Refunds are supported, and every payment is verified on the server.

    3:30 PMFeatures

    What it does, and why it matters.

    Everything here is in the app today.

    • Real availability

      Open times come from your working days and hours, daily break, days off, notice period, booking window and daily limit, so clients are never offered a time you cannot take.

    • No double bookings

      Every booking is checked again on the server, and the database itself refuses an overlap, even if two people tap the same time at the same moment.

    • Services that fit the work

      Each service has its own duration, an optional price and optional questions (some required) that the client answers while booking. Times are kept in your own time zone.

    • Confirmation your way

      Confirm instantly, or review each request first. You can confirm, decline, reschedule, cancel, complete or mark a no-show. Clients see where their request stands.

    • Changes with clear limits

      Clients can reschedule or cancel until a cut-off you set, with an optional reason the other side can read. After the cut-off they are pointed to your contact details.

    • Notifications that arrive

      Alerts appear in the app and on the phone, with a reminder an hour before an appointment. Each category can be switched on or off, and the phone uses one Slotify sound.

    • A page clients can trust

      A track record of bookings handled, completed, cancelled and rescheduled, with completion and cancellation rates. It is computed on the server, counts only finished bookings in its rates, and never includes client details.

    • Your day at a glance

      The dashboard shows what is left today, what is done, who is next, what is waiting for your reply, and your bookings over 30 days, the next 7 and the last 7, with a checklist for finishing your page.

    • Optional online payments

      Off until a professional turns them on. Payments run through Razorpay, with deposits and refunds, and are verified on the server. Setting this up needs your own Razorpay account and its activation.

    • Light, dark and calm motion

      Choose a Light, Dark or System theme, and standard, reduced or no animation. The reduced setting follows the phone’s own accessibility setting.

    • Control for the platform owner

      A separate admin console reviews professional applications, manages users and appointments, and oversees payments, payouts and refunds. Every action is written to an audit log.

    4:30 PMGood to know

    What Slotify is today, stated plainly.

    • Android only. There is no iPhone or web booking app yet.
    • Clients sign in. Booking needs an account, so bookings and notifications stay with the right person.
    • One practice, one professional. Staff members and branches are not part of the app yet.
    • One block of hours per day in setup, applied to the days you choose, with a daily break.
    • Payments need your own Razorpay account and its activation. Slotify works fully without them.

    4:45 PMQuestions

    Questions

    Who is Slotify for?

    Anyone who works by appointment: advocates, doctors, consultants, tutors, therapists, freelancers and small businesses. The scheduling engine knows nothing about any one profession. Wording, services, extra questions, hours and rules are settings.

    Do clients need an account to book?

    Yes. Clients sign in with an email address, find a professional by their link name, and book from the Book tab. Their bookings, notifications and profile live in that account.

    Can two people book the same time?

    No. Every booking is checked again on the server against your hours, breaks, days off, notice period and existing bookings, and the database refuses an overlapping booking even if two people tap the same time at the same moment.

    Can I approve each booking myself?

    Yes. You can confirm bookings instantly or review each request first. A request stays Pending until you confirm or decline it, and the client is notified.

    Can clients cancel or reschedule?

    Yes, until the cut-off you have set. After that they are asked to contact you. Either side can add a short reason, which the other person sees.

    Are online payments required?

    No. They are optional and off by default. You can turn them on, including deposits and refunds, after finishing payout setup with Razorpay.

    Which devices does it run on?

    Slotify is an Android app today.

    What does a client see about my track record?

    Counts only: bookings handled, completed, cancelled and rescheduled, with completion and cancellation rates. The rates count only bookings that have finished. No client details are ever included.

    Who builds Slotify?

    DEVELOPERS, the team behind Slotify. We design and build software that helps professionals run their day with less friction.
